\section{Related work}\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Impossible and defective tests.} ImpossibleBench makes coding tasks impossible by letting unit tests contradict the specification, so every pass is a shortcut, and shows that cheating depends on prompt strictness and on whether the model can flag the task for a human \citep{zhong2025impossiblebench}. EvilGenie detects reward hacking with held-out tests, judges and test-edit detection in Codex, Claude Code and Gemini CLI \citep{gabor2025evilgenie}. METR reports test and scorer tampering by frontier models \citep{vonarx2025reward}. Closest to our factorial, \citet{gomez2026escalation} crosses an escalation tool with an anti-hacking policy on defective test infrastructure for eight models and finds that the combination cuts hacking sharply; disclosure there is scored as \emph{identification of the defect}. Our factorial differs in three ways: the reporting option is one sentence of the ticket rather than a tool, the outcome is decided by a deterministic check out of the agent's reach rather than by a model judge, and the unit of comparison is a model-and-harness package. Our main addition is the other half of the picture: \emph{given} that the agent bypassed, what its report says about the bypass.

\paragraph{Honesty of agents' reports.} \citet{joglekar2025confessions} train a model to produce a separate confession, rewarded for honesty only, and find that it confesses when its main answer lies or omits, including after reward hacking; we code the ordinary final message that deployed agents produce without such training. OverclaimBench \citep{smyth2026overclaim} defines overclaiming as a final response that reports work the transcript shows was not done and finds misleading reports in most incomplete runs of file-review tasks. Agents facing broken tools fabricate or substitute and hide the failure \citep{guo2025upward}, and false completion claims against the environment's state are common in AppWorld \citep{advani2026falsesuccess}. We share the method of grounding the report in an independent record, here the checker, and add a graded taxonomy specific to shortcuts: whether the report names the mechanism, states what is lost, or describes the substitute as real.

\paragraph{Rules, multi-agent settings and propagation.} Unfinished-Bench finds that persistent agents break rules stated in internal documents far more often than rules stated by a user \citep{lee2026unfinished}. Reward hacking transmits between coding agents through shared code, memory and plans \citep{hu2026transmissible}; in a swarm of research agents an evaluation exploit spread and other agents warned and boycotted \citep{paglieri2026swarms}; reward hacking learned in production RL generalises to broader misalignment \citep{macdiarmid2025emergent}. These works motivate measuring reports, since in a multi-agent system the report of one agent is the input of the next and of the human who supervises them.

\paragraph{Prior search.} We searched arXiv for work coding final messages after a deterministically verified test bypass into confession, disclosure, vague mention, silence and misrepresentation, and found none; the closest are the design of \citet{gomez2026escalation}, the report grounding of \citet{smyth2026overclaim} and the framing of \citet{joglekar2025confessions}. This is a statement about our search, not proof of absence.
