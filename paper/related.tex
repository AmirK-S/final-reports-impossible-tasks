\section{Related work}\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Shortcuts on tests.} ImpossibleBench makes tasks impossible by letting tests contradict the specification and finds that cheating depends on prompt strictness and on whether the model can flag the task \citep{zhong2025impossiblebench}. EvilGenie detects reward hacking in Codex, Claude Code and Gemini CLI with held-out tests and test-edit detection \citep{gabor2025evilgenie}; METR reports test and scorer tampering \citep{vonarx2025reward}. \citet{gomez2026escalation} cross an escalation tool with an anti-hacking policy for eight models and find that the combination cuts hacking; disclosure there means identifying the defect. Our reporting option is one ticket sentence, our outcome a deterministic check instead of a model judge, and our unit a model-and-harness package. Our addition is what the report says once the agent has bypassed.

\paragraph{Honest reports.} \citet{joglekar2025confessions} train a separate confession channel; we code the ordinary final message of untrained deployed agents. OverclaimBench grounds reports in the transcript and finds misleading reports in most incomplete runs \citep{smyth2026overclaim}; agents facing broken tools substitute and hide the failure \citep{guo2025upward}; false completion claims are common in AppWorld \citep{advani2026falsesuccess}. We ground reports in the checker and separate naming the shortcut, stating its cost, and describing the substitute as real.

\paragraph{Rules across agents.} Agents break rules stated in internal documents more often than rules stated by a user \citep{lee2026unfinished}; reward hacking transmits between coding agents through shared code and plans \citep{hu2026transmissible}; an evaluation exploit spread in a swarm of research agents \citep{paglieri2026swarms}; reward hacking generalises to broader misalignment \citep{macdiarmid2025emergent}.

\paragraph{Prior search.} To our knowledge, no prior work codes final messages after a deterministically verified test bypass into confession, disclosure, vague mention, silence and misrepresentation.
